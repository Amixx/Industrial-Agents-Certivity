# spec: Front Bumper with towing eye and registration plate bracket

## Product Type

Front bumper — exterior front-end protective assembly with a removable towing eye and a front registration plate bracket.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — passenger car.

## Function

- Low-speed impact protection (front protective device)
- Pedestrian protection (lower leg impact zone)
- Towing attachment point (screw-in towing eye)
- Front registration plate mounting
- Aerodynamic and styling cover

## Main Parts

- Bumper cover (fascia)
- Energy absorber (foam)
- Bumper beam with threaded towing eye socket
- Removable towing eye
- Towing eye cover
- Front registration plate bracket
- Lower grille

## Materials

| Component                 | Typical material(s)          |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Bumper cover              | PP/EPDM-T20, painted         |
| Energy absorber           | EPP foam                     |
| Bumper beam               | Aluminium extrusion or HSS   |
| Towing eye                | Forged steel, zinc-plated    |
| Registration plate bracket| PP-T20                       |
| Lower grille              | ABS / PC-ABS                 |
