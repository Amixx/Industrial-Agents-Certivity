# Front bumper labels: UN R127 and UN GTR 9

Product: plain front bumper for an M1 car (cover, EPP absorber, beam, lower grille, towing eye cover). Its only R127 function is the legform-to-bumper test. Headform tests (bonnet top, windscreen, A-pillars) cover parts the bumper does not have. This version of R127 and GTR 9 has no upper legform to bonnet leading edge test, so that question does not come up.

## UN R127

- **Whole regulation: cited.** The bumper's stiffness, geometry and lead decide the legform results, which makes it the main component for this vehicle-level requirement.
- **1 Scope, 2 Definitions, 3 Application, 4 Approval, 6 Modifications, 8 to 11: not cited.** These are administrative paragraphs and definitions. The bumper definitions (2.11 to 2.14, 2.17, 2.25) matter, but the calibration rules say definitions are not cited.
- **5.1, 5.1.1: cited.** 5.1 picks the impactor by lower bumper height. 5.1.1 gives the FlexPLI limits: MCL 22 mm, ACL/PCL 13 mm, tibia 340 Nm, and 380 Nm in the relaxation zone.
- **5.1.2: cited, unsure.** These are the upper legform to bumper limits (7.5 kN, 510 Nm). They apply only when the lower bumper height is 425 mm or more, and the spec gives no height.
- **5.2 Headform: not cited.** It covers the bonnet top and windscreen.
- **5.3 ARHSS: not cited.** It applies only to vehicles with adjustable ride height. 5.3, 5.3.2 and 5.3.3 are marked unsure because they would re-apply the legform limits.
- **7 Conformity of production: not cited.** 7.2 only points back to paragraph 5 and has no number of its own, so it is marked unsure.
- **Annex 3 General test conditions: cited.**
  - 1.1: temperature and humidity.
  - 2.1: test site slope of 1 % or less (unsure).
  - 3.1.1: test in normal ride attitude (unsure).
  - 3.1.2: the cut-body includes the full front structure (unsure).
  - 3.2 (active devices) and 3.3 (parts with several positions) are not cited, as unsure. A plain bumper has neither.
- **Annex 4 Impactor specifications: not cited.** These are lab equipment specifications.
- **Annex 5 section 1, flexible lower legform: these test parameters are cited.**
  - 1.4: test points in the bumper test area.
  - 1.5: at least three tests, at least 84 mm apart.
  - 1.6: direction within ±2°.
  - 1.7: impactor bottom 75 mm above the ground.
  - 1.12: impact speed 11.1 m/s.
  - Not cited: flesh and skin, storage, free flight, yaw and position tolerances, the pre-impact tibia moment and offset compensation. These are lab set-up. 1.8 to 1.11 and 1.13 are marked unsure.
- **Annex 5 section 2, upper legform: 2.4 to 2.7 cited, unsure.** These are the same kinds of parameters as in section 1, but they depend on whether 5.1.2 applies.
- **Annex 5 sections 3 to 5 (headform) and Annex 6 (impactor certification): not cited.**

## UN GTR 9

- **Verdict: not cited (unsure).** Under the 1958 Agreement the approval basis is R127, which transposes GTR 9 and sets the same limits. An expert writing the requirement list for UNECE type approval cites R127 and not the GTR it came from. Some experts might still list GTR 9 for markets that transpose it nationally.
- **Paragraphs, labelled as if GTR 9 were cited, to match R127:**
  - 4.1 is cited. 4.1.1 and 4.1.2 send each test to its impactor and procedure; they are cited, unsure.
  - 5.1.1 is cited; 5.1.2 is cited, unsure.
  - 6.1.1 is cited; 6.1.2, 6.2.1.1 and 6.2.1.2 are cited, unsure.
  - 7.1.1.1 to 7.1.1.4 (bumper test area, direction, 75 mm height, 11.1 m/s) are cited.
  - 7.1.2.1 to 7.1.2.3 (upper legform) are cited, unsure.
- **Not cited:**
  - Purpose and scope, definitions, and the 4.2/4.3 and 5.2 headform paragraphs.
  - DPPS (6.2.4 and Annex 2 HIT simulation).
  - 6.3 impactor specifications and 8 impactor certification.
  - 7.2 to 7.4 headform procedures.
  - The 7.1.1 and 7.1.2 heading rows, whose own text is about impactor handling (unsure).
  - The lab tolerances in 7.1.1.3.1 to 7.1.1.3.4 and 7.1.1.5 (unsure).
