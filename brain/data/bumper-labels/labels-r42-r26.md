# Front bumper labels: UN R42 and UN R26

Product: plain front bumper of an M1 car (cover, EPP absorber, beam, lower grille, towing eye cover; no lamps, no sensors).

| Regulation | Cited | Total rows | Unsure (sure = n) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UN R42 | 18 | 90 | 13 |
| UN R26 | 19 | 193 | 20 |

## UN R42 (low-speed impact, front and rear protective devices)

- Whole regulation: cited. The bumper is the front protective device the regulation tests.
- 1-5 (scope, purpose, definitions, application, approval and marking): not cited. 3.1.5 (vehicle corner) and 3.1.6 (reference height) are definitions that bound the protected zone; marked unsure for 3.1.6, which also says the vehicle "should embody sufficient protective devices" at that height.
- 6.1 and 6.1.1-6.1.5: cited. These are the pass criteria after the impact tests, vehicle-level outcomes the bumper determines (bonnet operable, no cooling leaks, steering and suspension intact). 6.1.1 (lamps) and 6.1.4 (exhaust) are unsure: the bumper has no lamps, and the exhaust mostly matters for the rear impact.
- 7-11 (modifications, CoP, penalties, discontinued, technical services): not cited. 8.1 marked unsure since it singles out the features that influence impact behaviour, but it has no number.
- Annex 3: cited where a parameter is fixed for the product: 2.3.5 (bumper between corners must be intersected by the reference-line plane in both load states), 2.3.6/2.7.3/2.8.2 (445 mm), 2.7.5 (4 km/h), 2.8.4 (2.5 km/h), plus 2.3.3 (impactor mass), 2.7.1/2.7.2 and 2.8.1/2.8.3 (impact count, load states, 300 mm spacing, 60 ± 5° corner angle), which are less certain. Testing ground, vehicle state, impactor construction, pendulum/barrier set-up, measuring accuracy and equivalent methods are not cited. 3.1 and 3.2 (repair between tests, recovery time for self-restoring materials like the EPP absorber) are unsure.

## UN R26 (external projections of M1 vehicles)

- Whole regulation: cited. The bumper cover, lower grille and towing eye cover form part of the front external surface.
- 1-4 (scope, definitions, application, approval, marks, separate technical units): not cited. 2.11/2.12 (bumper, bumper cover) and 3.1.2.2 (dimensioned bumper drawings) are bumper-specific, so they are marked unsure.
- 5 General specifications: 5.2, 5.3, 5.4 cited as the core requirements for the whole front-end surface. 5.1/5.1.1 (exempt zones: above 2 m, below floor line, not contactable by a 100 mm sphere) cited but unsure, since they set the zone the rules apply to rather than a requirement. 5.5 (≤ 60 Shore A alternative) cited but unsure, because the PP/EPDM fascia and ABS grille are harder than that. 5.6 not cited.
- 6 Particular specifications: cited 6.3.1/6.3.2 (grilles and gaps, for the lower grille), 6.5.1-6.5.4 (bumpers: turned-in ends, 5 mm / 2.5 mm radii in the 15° zone, < 5 mm insets, bumper cover exemption) and 6.9.1 (folds in body panels, which applies to fascia character lines; unsure). Not cited: headlights, wipers, handles/filler caps, wheels, deflectors, jacking/exhaust, roof, windows, racks and aerials, which belong to other parts of the car. Unsure, conditional on parts the spec does not list: 6.1.1/6.1.2 (badges or protective strips on the bumper), 6.8.1 (an exposed metal edge), 6.12.1 (active grille shutters), 6.15.1 (front registration plate bracket on the bumper).
- 7-12 (modifications, CoP, penalties, discontinued, technical services, transitional): not cited.
- Annex 3: cited the measuring methods behind the cited limits: 1.1-1.3 (fold height H with 165/100 mm circles, for 6.9.1), 2.2 (projection via 100 mm sphere, for insets and the towing eye cover) and 4.1 (grille gap measurement, required by 6.3.1). All are unsure. Headlamp visor method and drawing provision 1.4 not cited.
- Annex 4 (communication form for separate technical units): not cited.
