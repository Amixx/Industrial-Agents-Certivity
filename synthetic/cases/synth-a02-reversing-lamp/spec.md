# spec: Reversing Lamp

## Product Type

Reversing lamp — standalone rear lighting device that illuminates the road behind the vehicle and warns other road users that the vehicle is reversing or about to reverse.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — passenger car. UNECE lighting regulations referenced apply across M and N categories.

## Function

- Reversing illumination (white light to the rear)
- Light transmission & color definition (outer lens)

Fitted as a pair, one on each side of the rear bumper fascia. No other lighting or signalling function is integrated in this device.

## Main Parts

- Reversing lamp
- Clear outer lens
- Housing
- Electrical connector
