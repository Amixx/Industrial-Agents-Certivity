# spec: Rear Retro-Reflector

## Product Type

Rear retro-reflector — passive, non-triangular device that returns light from other vehicles' headlamps towards its source. No light source and no electrical connection.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — passenger car. UNECE lighting regulations referenced apply across M and N categories.

## Function

- Passive conspicuity (retro-reflection of incident light)
- Colour definition (red)

Two units are fitted, one at each rear corner of the bumper. Class IA device; the optical unit and the red filter are moulded as one part.

## Main Parts

- Retro-reflecting optical unit (moulded prism optics)
- Backing plate
- Mounting clips
